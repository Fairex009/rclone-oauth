Privacy Policy

Last updated: 23 September 2026

FAIREX rclone is a personal-use OAuth application used only by its owner to connect rclone to the owner's own Google Drive.

Google data accessed

The application may authorize rclone to access Google Drive files, folders, and related metadata as required to provide the configured rclone mount.

How the data is used

Google Drive data is used only for the owner's local rclone operations, including listing, reading, creating, modifying, moving, and deleting files when the owner performs those actions through the mounted drive.

Storage and sharing

This website does not receive or store Google Drive files, OAuth access tokens, or refresh tokens. OAuth credentials and tokens used by rclone are handled by rclone on the owner's device according to the owner's local rclone configuration.

No Google user data is sold, rented, or shared with advertisers or unrelated third parties by this application.

Data retention

The website retains no Google Drive user data. Any local cache or configuration created by rclone is controlled by the owner on the owner's device.

Revoking access

The owner can revoke the application's Google account access at any time from the Google Account security settings and can remove the rclone configuration from the local device.

Contact

This is a private personal-use application. The Google OAuth consent screen lists the owner's support/contact email for account-related questions.
